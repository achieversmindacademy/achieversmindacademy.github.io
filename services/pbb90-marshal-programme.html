About PBB90

Bijak Belajar 90 Hari (PBB90) is a holistic student development programme designed to help school students improve their learning skills, memory, discipline, confidence, emotional development and life skills.

Developed and conducted by Achievers Mind Academy, the programme goes beyond traditional academic teaching.

Instead of teaching the school syllabus itself, PBB90 focuses on teaching students practical techniques to understand, remember and apply what they learn.

Why PBB90?

Students today face challenges that go beyond academic performance.

They need skills to manage:

  • Studies
  • Memory
  • Time
  • Emotions
  • Relationships
  • Responsibilities
  • Goals
  • Daily habits

PBB90 addresses these areas through structured learning and personal development.

Two Core Approaches

STUDY SKILLS

Students learn practical techniques to improve how they study, understand information, remember facts and prepare for examinations.

LIFE SKILLS

Students develop positive habits, self-discipline, time management, confidence, responsibility and better decision-making.

Parents are also encouraged to understand and support their children's learning environment at home.

Core Areas

MEMORY & COGNITIVE SKILLS

SMART LEARNING

Practical study and learning techniques.

TIME MANAGEMENT

Building discipline and better use of time.

POSITIVE HABITS

Developing positive daily habits and life skills.

EMOTIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Supporting emotional awareness and personal development.

GOAL SETTING

CHARACTER & VALUES

Developing responsibility, values and personal growth.

The Marshal Programme

Learning Support Beyond the Classroom

The Marshal Programme is an important part of PBB90.

It creates a support system connecting:

School + Student + Family + Community

Selected Marshal officers may include:

  • PIBG members
  • Alumni
  • Teachers
  • Interested parents

Marshals are trained and guided by Master Alagan Govindan and support students throughout the 90-day programme.

What Do Marshals Do?

🏠 HOME VISITS

Marshals conduct home visits to understand and support the student's learning environment.

👨‍👩‍👧 PARENT DIALOGUE

Communicating with parents and discussing the student's development.

👩‍🏫 TEACHER COMMUNICATION

Working with teachers and the school.

📖 STUDY MONITORING

Monitoring study and learning habits.

🌱 CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

Supporting positive habits and personal growth.

🎯 GOAL SUPPORT

Encouraging students to develop and work towards positive goals.

🥗 HEALTHY HABITS

Guidance around nutrition and positive lifestyle habits.

📊 PROGRESS MONITORING

Monitoring and reporting programme progress.

🤝 SCHOOL / PIBG COORDINATION

Monthly coordination with the school/PIBG.

At least four home visits during the 90-day programme.

The 90-Day Journey

PHASE 1 — PREPARATION & LAUNCH

  • School approval
  • Student selection
  • Marshal selection
  • Marshal briefing
  • Parent briefing
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Official launch

PHASE 2 — STUDENT DEVELOPMENT

Structured Bijak Belajar learning sessions covering memory, learning techniques, discipline, time management, positive habits and personal development.

PHASE 3 — MARSHAL MONITORING

  • Home visits
  • Student observation
  • Parent dialogue
  • Ongoing guidance

PHASE 4 — PARENT ENGAGEMENT

Seminars and dialogue sessions helping parents support their children's learning.

PHASE 5 — EVALUATION & COMPLETION

  • Post-programme assessment
  • Certificate Presentation Ceremony

Partnership Model

SCHOOL

Permission, coordination and teacher support.

ACHIEVERS MIND ACADEMY

Conducts learning sessions, trains and guides Marshals, and provides parent development sessions.

MARSHALS

Monitor students, visit homes, communicate with parents and provide guidance.

STUDENTS

Participate actively, practise techniques and develop positive habits.

PARENTS

Support the student's learning journey and work together with the school and Marshals.

Become more organised
Improve time management
Build confidence
Develop positive attitudes
Develop emotional awareness
Create positive daily habits
Become more independent
Become more responsible
