The programme

Super Memory Programme

A structured training experience designed to help students practise memory, focus, learning skills and confidence — through guided sessions, live demonstrations and practical techniques.

Memory Retention

Practical techniques for holding and keeping information — the foundation of the method.

Recall

Training the ability to bring information back quickly and accurately when it is needed.

Concentration

Focused-attention practice built through guided activities and live exercises.

Learning Techniques

Structured approaches to studying, absorbing and organising new material.

Visualisation

Mental-imagery methods used throughout the memory work students practise.

Confidence

Progress earned through practice — and demonstrated in front of parents and peers.
