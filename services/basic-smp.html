A Free Introduction to Super Memory Programme

Basic SMP is designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of AMA's Super Memory Programme through accessible, practical activities. The programme gives schools an opportunity to expose students to memory and concentration training and allows students to experience the methodology before deciding whether they want to pursue more extensive training.
