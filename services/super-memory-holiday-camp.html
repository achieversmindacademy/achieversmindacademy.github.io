About the Camp

Achievers Mind Academy's Super Memory Holiday Camp is a yearly event created to give children and youth an opportunity to learn, explore, build friendships and discover their potential in a safe and fun environment.

The camp combines memory and brain-engaging activities with outdoor experiences, games, leadership challenges, teamwork and personal development.

It is not simply an academic programme.

It is an experience where children can learn while having fun.

Camp Activities

🧠 BRAIN ACTIVITIES

Challenges designed to engage memory, thinking and concentration.

🗺 TREASURE HUNT

Team-based challenges requiring communication and problem-solving.

🔥 CAMPFIRE

A shared camp experience encouraging friendship and connection.

🏊 SWIMMING

Recreation, relaxation and fun.

🎯 LEADERSHIP

Activities that encourage responsibility and confidence.

🎮 FUN GAMES

Games and challenges designed to make learning enjoyable.

🤝 TEAM ACTIVITIES

Building communication, trust and collaboration.

💪 MIND & BODY

Activities that encourage physical participation alongside mental engagement.

Life Skills

COMMUNICATION

Ability to express ideas clearly and listen to team members.

COLLABORATION

Willingness to support peers, share tasks and build group trust.

PROBLEM-SOLVING

Skill in handling stress, adapting to sudden changes and making smart choices.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Readiness to accept responsibility for group outcomes and personal actions.

Character Counts

The camp reflects four important values:

CARING

HONESTY

RESPECT

RESPONSIBILITY

These values should be shown through daily camp life and activities.
