Programme overview

Beyond Traditional Academic Teaching

Bijak Belajar 90 Hari (PBB90) is a holistic student development programme designed to help school students improve their learning skills, memory, discipline, confidence, emotional development and life skills.

Developed and conducted by Achievers Mind Academy, the programme goes beyond traditional academic teaching. Instead of teaching the school syllabus itself, PBB90 focuses on teaching students practical techniques to understand, remember and apply what they learn.

The 90 days connect three worlds that shape every student's success: structured learning sessions in school, a supportive environment at home, and guidance from a trained Marshal who walks alongside the student and family throughout the journey.

Why PBB90? Students today need skills to manage:
  • Studies
  • Memory
  • Time
  • Emotions
  • Relationships
  • Responsibilities
  • Goals
  • Daily habits

Two core approaches

Study Skills + Life Skills

APPROACH 01

Study Skills

Students learn practical techniques to improve how they study, understand information, remember facts and prepare for examinations.

APPROACH 02

Life Skills

Students develop positive habits, self-discipline, time management, confidence, responsibility and better decision-making. Parents are also encouraged to understand and support their children's learning environment at home.

Who are the marshals?

Selected Marshal officers may include PIBG members, alumni, teachers and interested parents. Marshals are trained and guided by Master Alagan Govindan and support students throughout the entire 90-day programme.

4+
Home visits per student At least four home visits take place during the 90-day programme.

What do marshals do?

  • Home visits to understand the learning environment
  • Parent dialogue on the student's development
  • Teacher communication & school liaison
  • Study habit monitoring
  • Character development support
  • Lifestyle & nutrition guidance
  • Goal-setting support
  • Progress reporting
  • Monthly school / PIBG coordination

Who PBB90 Is For

The programme can be adapted from Year 4 up to university level. For school proposals, PBB90 targets 60 selected Year 4–6 students, with particular focus on students from the B40 community.
