About preview sessions

Questions about the preview.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. The preview session is completely free — for parents and children. Its purpose is to let you experience the method and understand the programme before any decision is made.

Should my child attend?

Yes. The preview is designed for parents and children together. Your child will take part in the hands-on activities — it is the best way for both of you to understand the method.

What age is the programme for?

The Super Memory Programme is designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5.

Will I be pressured to enrol?

No. The preview exists so families can understand the programme with confidence. Whether you enrol is entirely your decision — and many families take time to decide.
