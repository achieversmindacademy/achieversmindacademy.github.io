About MMCP

The Memory Mastery Certification Programme is designed for students who want to go significantly deeper into the Super Memory Programme.

Unlike a short programme, MMCP is structured as a long-term training journey lasting approximately 6 months, with sessions conducted every Saturday and Sunday.

Students progressively work through memory techniques, SMP modules and more advanced training experiences.

The programme is designed to create consistency through regular practice and long-term development.

100% MONEY BACK GUARANTEE

We stand behind our training with complete confidence. If you don't see measurable improvement in your child's memory and learning abilities after completing the programme, we'll refund your investment.

What Students Will Learn

FULL SMP MODULE TRAINING

Students work through the wider range of Super Memory Programme modules and techniques.

BLINDFOLD / MIDBRAIN TRAINING

The programme includes progressive blindfold-related training.

The programme aims to help students progress beyond Level 3 towards higher levels, including Levels 4–6.

Important: This is presented as AMA's training methodology, not as a scientifically proven neurological effect.

1,000 RANDOM WORDS

One of the programme's targets is to train students towards remembering:

1,000

random words by the end of the programme.

Presented as a programme target rather than a guaranteed result.

GENIUS MIND LEARNING STATE

The training combines memory techniques, exercises and activities designed to encourage concentration, focus and deeper learning.
