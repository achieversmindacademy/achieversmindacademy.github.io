What happens at a preview

A session you experience, not just watch.

Live Demonstrations

Watch students perform memory techniques, recall challenges and recognition activities — live.

Hands-On Activities

Parents and children try the activities themselves — the method is experienced, not just explained.

Method Introduction

A clear introduction to the Super Memory Programme — what it builds, how it works and how sessions are run.

Your Decision

Leave with a full understanding of the programme — and decide, together with your child, whether to enrol.

The full journey

From first experience to demonstration.

1

Free Preview

Your first introduction. Watch the demonstrations, try the activities and understand the method — free, with no commitment.

2

Foundation Techniques

Session by session, students are introduced to the core memory and learning techniques through guided practice.

3

Applied Practice

Techniques are applied to real learning material — the kind students meet in school every day.

4

Live Demonstration

Students demonstrate their progress live, in front of parents — progress you can see with your own eyes.

5

Confidence & Continuation

Students leave with techniques to keep using — and the confidence that comes from having used them for real.
