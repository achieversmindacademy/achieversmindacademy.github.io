Why Achievers Mind Academy is different

More than a memory class.

Achievers Mind Academy is not a tuition centre. It is a specialised cognitive and memory training academy with a distinctive live methodology — and the best way to understand it is to see it in action.

01

One discipline, mastered

We do one thing — memory and learning skills — refined over more than three decades. No distractions, no shortcuts.

02

Trained by the founder

Master Alagan Govindan — MSc-trained and 33+ years in practice — still teaches, demonstrates and leads the programmes himself.

03

Progress you can see

Nothing is taken on faith. Techniques are demonstrated live, by students, in front of parents — every step of the way.

04

Proven under pressure

The programme

Super Memory Programme

A structured training experience designed to help students practise memory, focus, learning skills and confidence — through guided sessions, live demonstrations and practical techniques.

Memory Retention

Practical techniques for holding and keeping information — the foundation of the method.

Recall

Training the ability to bring information back quickly and accurately when it is needed.

Concentration

Focused-attention practice built through guided activities and live exercises.

Learning Techniques

Structured approaches to studying, absorbing and organising new material.

Visualisation

Mental-imagery methods used throughout the memory work students practise.

Confidence

Progress earned through practice — and demonstrated in front of parents and peers.
