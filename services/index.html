Programmes That Build More Than Memory

Achievers Mind Academy offers a range of memory, learning and personal development programmes designed for different stages of a learner's journey. From free introductory programmes for Tamil schools to our core Super Memory Programme, intensive Memory Mastery Certification Programme, yearly holiday camp, adult memory training and the 90-day Bijak Belajar student development programme, each experience is designed around learning, practice, participation and personal growth.

01 BASIC SMP
FREE SCHOOL PROGRAMME

Basic Super Memory Programme

A Free Introduction to Memory & Learning

Basic SMP is a free introductory programme offered to Tamil schools. The purpose of Basic SMP is to give students an opportunity to experience the fundamentals of Super Memory Programme training and to introduce them to simple practices that can support concentration, focus and positive learning habits. Students are introduced to SMP Yoga as well as the 10 Secrets — 10 Habits to Improve and Protect Your Brain. This is intended to be an accessible starting point for Tamil schools that want their students to experience Basic SMP without paying for the programme.

90-DAY PROGRAMME

Bijak Belajar 90 Hari

PBB90 Marshal Programme

Bijak Belajar 90 Hari (PBB90) is a holistic 90-day student development programme designed to help school students improve their learning skills, memory, discipline, confidence, emotional development and life skills. Developed and conducted by Achievers Mind Academy, the programme goes beyond traditional academic teaching. Instead of teaching the school syllabus itself, the programme focuses on helping students develop techniques to understand, remember and apply what they learn.

"We do not teach the school syllabus. We teach techniques to remember the syllabus."
— Master Alagan Govindan
