The programme

A complete training pathway.

The Super Memory Programme is a structured training experience for students from Standard 4 to Form 5 — taught in small groups with live demonstrations and hands-on practice.

Reserve Your Free Preview

What you'll train

Six areas of the programme.

Each area is trained through guided activities and live demonstrations — so students see the technique in action before they practise it themselves.

Memory Retention & Recall

Holding information and bringing it back accurately when needed — through practised, structured techniques.

Concentration & Focus

Focused-attention training built into every session, so students learn to direct their minds deliberately.

Learning Techniques

Structured approaches to studying and absorbing new material — practical skills for school and beyond.

Visualisation

Mental-imagery methods that form the backbone of the memory work students practise.

Live Demonstration

Techniques are shown in action — by trainers and by students — so progress is visible, not assumed.

Confidence Through Practice

Students build confidence by doing — performing techniques and demonstrating them in front of parents and peers.

How it works

Five sessions, one clear pathway.

The programme is structured as a short, intensive training pathway — each session building on the last.

1

Free Preview

Your first introduction. Watch the demonstrations, try the activities and understand the method — free, with no commitment.

2

Foundation Techniques

Session by session, students are introduced to the core memory and learning techniques through guided practice.

3

Applied Practice

Techniques are applied to real learning material — the kind students meet in school every day.

4

Live Demonstration

Students demonstrate their progress live, in front of parents — progress you can see with your own eyes.

5

Confidence & Continuation

Students leave with techniques to keep using — and the confidence that comes from having used them for real.

Who it's for

For students who want to learn better.

The Super Memory Programme is designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5 — the years when study habits, exam techniques and learning confidence take shape.

Parents are part of the journey from the start. Preview sessions are free, demonstrations are live, and you are invited to see the method in action before any commitment is made.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What happens in a free preview?

The preview is a live session where you and your child watch demonstrations, try the activities and see how the programme works — completely free, with no obligation to enrol.

Who can join the programme?

The Super Memory Programme is designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5.

How is the programme structured?

The programme is a structured pathway of sessions — foundation techniques first, then applied practice, then live demonstration — so progress is visible at every stage.

Do parents attend?

Yes — parents are part of the journey from the very beginning. Previews are free, and demonstrations are held live so parents can see results with their own eyes.

Where are the programmes held?

Preview programmes and events have been hosted at venues across Malaysia — including YMCA Brickfields, Digital Wave Academy Klang, and Sri Subramaniar Paripalana Saba Johor.
