Programme overview

A Long-Term Training Journey

The Memory Mastery Certification Programme is designed for students who want to go significantly deeper into the Super Memory Programme.

Unlike a short programme, MMCP is structured as a long-term training journey of approximately six months, with sessions conducted every Saturday and Sunday.

Students progressively work through memory techniques, SMP modules and more advanced training experiences — building consistency through regular practice and long-term development.

What students will learn

Deeper Training, Higher Levels

01

Full SMP Modules

Students work through the complete range of Super Memory Programme modules and techniques.

02

Advanced Blindfold Progression

Progressive blindfold-related training aiming beyond Level 3 towards Levels 4–6 — presented as AMA's training methodology.

03

1,000 Random Words Target

A programme target of remembering up to 1,000 random words by the end of the training.

04

Genius Mind Learning State

Exercises and activities designed to encourage concentration, focus and deeper learning states.
