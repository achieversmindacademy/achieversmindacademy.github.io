The programme

Super Memory Programme.

A structured training experience designed to help students practise memory, focus, learning skills and confidence. Designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5 — and for the parents who support them.

What the programme develops

Skills you can see practised.

The Super Memory Programme is built around practical techniques and guided practice. These are the skills students work on, session by session.

Memory Retention

Practical techniques for holding and keeping information — the foundation of the method.

Recall

Training the ability to bring information back quickly and accurately when it is needed.

Concentration

Focused-attention practice built through guided activities and live exercises.

Learning Techniques

Structured approaches to studying, absorbing and organising new material.

Visualisation

Mental-imagery methods used throughout the memory work students practise.

Confidence

Progress earned through practice — and demonstrated in front of parents and peers.

The student journey

How the programme works.

Every family begins the same way — by experiencing the method. From there, the journey is clear and guided.

01

Free Preview

Parents and students attend a free preview — live demonstrations, hands-on activities and an introduction to the method.

02

Enrolment

Families who have seen the method decide whether it fits their child — with full understanding of the programme.

03

Training

Guided sessions in which students learn and practise memory techniques, focus work and learning methods.

04

Practice

Students apply the techniques — building recall, concentration and confidence through regular practice.

05

Demonstration

Progress is shown live — students demonstrate what they have learned in front of parents, trainers and peers.

Who it is for

Students. Supported by parents.

The programme is designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5 — the school years where learning demands grow quickly and good mental habits pay off most.

Parents are part of the journey from the start. The free preview is attended by parents and children together, so families understand exactly what their child will practise before any decision is made.

Questions parents ask

Before the preview.

Who is the programme for?

The Super Memory Programme is designed for students from Standard 4 to Form 5. Parents attend the free preview with their child and are part of the journey throughout.

How do we start?

Every family starts with a free preview session. You watch live demonstrations, try the activities yourself and get a clear introduction to the method — then decide if the programme fits your child.

Do parents attend the preview?

Yes. The preview is attended by parents and children together. Understanding the method is part of the experience — parents are expected to see what their child will practise.

Is this a tuition centre?

No. Achievers Mind Academy is a specialised memory and learning skills training academy. The programme develops the mental skills behind learning — memory, recall, concentration and confidence — through guided practice and live demonstration.

Are previews available outside Selangor?

Preview programmes and events have been conducted across Malaysia — including Johor, Perak and Pahang. Register your interest for the next session in your area and our team will contact you.

The next step

See the demonstrations. Try the activities. Understand the method. Then decide — with your child, with confidence.
