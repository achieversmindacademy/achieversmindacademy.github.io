Programme overview

More Than A Camp

The Super Memory Holiday Camp is AMA's yearly event created for children and youth to experience learning in a completely different environment.

The camp combines memory and brain-engaging activities with outdoor fun, games, teamwork, leadership development, friendship and personal growth.

It is not simply about studying. Children get the opportunity to participate, explore, challenge themselves, make friends and experience activities that engage both mind and body.

Camp life

Days Full Of Discovery

  • Treasure hunt
  • Campfire night
  • Swimming
  • Brain-engaging activities
  • Leadership activities
  • Fun games
  • Teamwork challenges

Every activity is designed around a purpose — building communication, trust, resilience and confidence while making memories that last long after the camp ends.
