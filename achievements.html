On the world stage

World-record performances by our students.

These are our students. Every one of them was trained at Achievers Mind Academy — under Master Alagan Govindan — to identify objects, colours, images and surroundings while blindfolded. The records they set are the result of that training.

Guinness World Record
Guinness World Records Our Student

Punithamalar Rajashekar

Ipoh, Perak · 10 years old at the time of the record

“Fastest time to arrange a chess set blindfolded — 45.72 seconds.”

Punithamalar is our student — a young Malaysian prodigy who arranged a complete chess set while blindfolded in 45.72 seconds, officially recognised by Guinness World Records and breaking the previous world record of 1 minute 2 seconds.

A student of SJK(T) Menglembu, Ipoh, she is a former Kids Got Talent Malaysia contestant, a recipient of Asia's Outstanding Child Award, and an active FIDE-registered chess player who aims to become a space scientist.

World Book of Records
World Book of Records AMBR National Record Our Student

Oviya Sargunan

Rawang, Selangor · The blindfolded rollerblading slalom record

“Blindfolded rollerblading slalom across 10 zigzag cones — in 10 seconds flat.”

Oviya is our student from Rawang, Selangor. Wearing a blindfold, she rollerbladed a slalom through 10 randomly placed cones in an astonishing 10 seconds flat — an extraordinary feat certified by the World Book of Records on 15 June 2026.

She is also an AMBR national record holder — recognised as the first female with the fastest time to slalom across 10 obstacles on rollerblades while blindfolded (Under 16).
