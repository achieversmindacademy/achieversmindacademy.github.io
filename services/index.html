01Free School Programme

Basic SMP

A free introduction to memory & learning

Basic SMP is a free introductory programme offered to Tamil schools. It gives students the opportunity to experience the fundamentals of Super Memory Programme training and introduces simple practices that support concentration, focus and positive learning habits — an accessible starting point for schools that want their students to experience AMA training.

02Core Programme

Super Memory Programme

Our core 3-month memory development programme

The Super Memory Programme (SMP) is AMA's regular core programme. Over three months, students learn and practise a wide range of memory techniques — going far beyond a simple introduction. Training is practical: students participate in activities involving memory, concentration, learning techniques, blindfold-related training and progressive recognition activities.

Techniques included

  • Practical training

Students don't simply sit and listen — they practise, participate and are challenged. Live demonstrations, hands-on experience, understand the method, decide with confidence.

Take memory training further
