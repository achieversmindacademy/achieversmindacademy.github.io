Programme overview

A First Step Into The Super Memory Programme

Basic SMP is designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of AMA's Super Memory Programme through accessible, practical activities.

The programme gives schools an opportunity to expose students to memory and concentration training, and allows students to experience the methodology before deciding whether they want to pursue more extensive training such as the full Super Memory Programme.

Everything is delivered inside the school environment, making it an easy starting point for schools that want their students to experience AMA training without any cost.

What students experience

Simple Practices. Real Foundations.

01

SMP Yoga

A series of gentle movements and breathing exercises that help students prepare for learning, develop awareness, concentration and focus.

Introduction to SMP

Students get a first-hand introduction to the Super Memory Programme and how AMA's training works.

Simple, engaging memory exercises that show students that remembering can be trained.
