About the programme

Basic SMP is an introductory programme offered free of charge to Tamil schools.

The programme provides students with an opportunity to experience basic Super Memory Programme practices and learn simple approaches that encourage concentration, focus and positive learning habits.

It is designed as a starting point for schools that want to introduce their students to memory-development activities without requiring them to commit to a long-term programme.

What makes it work

  • Small-group, trainer-led sessions
  • Live demonstrations students can see and copy
  • Parents invited to the free preview

What Students Will Experience

SMP YOGA

Students are introduced to basic SMP Yoga practices as part of the programme.

The activities are designed to help students prepare themselves for learning and develop greater awareness, concentration and focus.
