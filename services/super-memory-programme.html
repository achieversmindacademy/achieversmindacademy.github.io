About SMP

The Super Memory Programme is AMA's regular core programme for students who want to explore memory development, concentration and practical learning techniques in greater depth.

Over three months, students are introduced to a range of SMP modules and techniques through structured sessions and practical activities.

The programme is designed to be experienced rather than simply read about.

That is why AMA encourages interested students and parents to participate in a preview session before deciding to join.

Programme Highlights

3 MONTHS

Structured programme journey with progressive skill development.

SMP YOGA

Concentration and preparation activities for focused learning.

BLINDFOLD TRAINING

Introduction to blindfold-based training techniques.

LEVELS 1–3

Progressive blindfold recognition training stages.
