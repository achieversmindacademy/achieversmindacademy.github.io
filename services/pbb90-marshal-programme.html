The Marshal Programme

Learning Support Beyond the Classroom

The Marshal Programme is an important part of PBB90.

It creates a support system connecting:

School + Student + Family + Community

Selected Marshal officers may include:

  • PIBG members
  • Alumni
  • Teachers
  • Interested parents

Marshals are trained and guided by Master Alagan Govindan and support students throughout the 90-day programme.

What Do Marshals Do?

🏠 HOME VISITS

Marshals conduct home visits to understand and support the student's learning environment.

👨‍👩‍👧 PARENT DIALOGUE

Communicating with parents and discussing the student's development.

👩‍🏫 TEACHER COMMUNICATION

Working with teachers and the school.

📖 STUDY MONITORING

Monitoring study and learning habits.

🌱 CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

Supporting positive habits and personal growth.

At least four home visits during the 90-day programme.

The 90-Day Journey

PHASE 1 — PREPARATION & LAUNCH

  • School approval
  • Student selection
  • Marshal selection
  • Marshal briefing
  • Parent briefing
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Official launch

PHASE 2 — STUDENT DEVELOPMENT

Structured Bijak Belajar learning sessions covering memory, learning techniques, discipline, time management, positive habits and personal development.

PHASE 3 — MARSHAL MONITORING

  • Home visits
  • Student observation
  • Parent dialogue
  • Ongoing guidance

PHASE 4 — PARENT ENGAGEMENT

Seminars and dialogue sessions helping parents support their children's learning.

PHASE 5 — EVALUATION & COMPLETION

  • Post-programme assessment
  • Certificate Presentation Ceremony

Partnership Model

Why Achievers Mind Academy?
